Pricing questions

What does commission on closure mean?

On the outbound model, alongside the monthly fee we agree a commission on deals that close from our pipeline. The percentage and terms are agreed in writing before we start.

Are tool costs included?

On GTM engineering, tools are billed in addition to the retainer, as required by your stack. We list every tool and cost before you commit. On the outbound model, we run it through our own process.

Is there a long contract?

It is a monthly subscription. We will share the notice terms in the proposal.

Can we start small and add channels later?

Yes. Most teams start with LinkedIn and email, then add calling or other channels once the first results are in.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can promise a number of meetings without knowing your market. We share what we expect after discovery and report against it weekly.

Want a quote for your setup?

Share your ICP and current stack. We will come back with a plan and a clear number.
