Frequently asked questions

Who is a good fit?

B2B agencies and SaaS teams with a clear buyer, an offer that is already working with some customers, and someone who can close the calls we book.

Who is not a good fit?

Teams still searching for product-market fit, or businesses selling mainly to consumers. Outbound works best when you know who you are selling to.

How do you measure success?

Replies, qualified meetings and pipeline value, reported weekly. We agree the targets after discovery.

Which markets do you work in?

We work with teams worldwide, including UK, Europe, Australia and the US, with a team based in Indore.

Do we need to buy tools ourselves?

On the outbound model, no. On GTM engineering, tool costs are passed through as required and listed up front.

Can you work alongside our SDRs?

Yes. We can run channels they do not have time for, or hand over processes and train your team.
