After a discovery call and a short ICP exercise we set up domains, lists and sequences. We will give you a concrete timeline on the call, based on your tooling and how much already exists.

No. We generate and qualify conversations, then hand booked calls to whoever closes for you. Some clients later have us hire and train their in-house team.

Will outreach hurt our brand or domain?

We send in small, verified batches from properly set-up infrastructure, and a person reviews copy before it goes out.
